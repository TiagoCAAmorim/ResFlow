# Test results of the final model

Results of every test rollout of the final 2D 1-day model: 65 test cases in two modes (imposed well volumes and the
rate model), 130 rollouts in all. Each case folder has the maps, the well volumes and the metrics; the CSV files in this
folder hold the per-rollout table and the summary tables of the paper.

- `all_rollouts.csv`: one row per case, metrics at day 1825 in both modes (the table of every test rollout of the paper's supplementary material).
- `summary.csv`: medians over the cases of a set with 95 % bootstrap intervals (the test-set and cumulative-error tables of the paper). Rows: set x mode x day (1000 and 1825). Cumulative errors only in the rate rows.
- `<set>/<case>/`: `perm.png`, `maps_imposed.png`, `maps_rate.png`, `wells_rate.png`, `metrics.csv`.

## Model, modes and sets

The state model is the final architecture of the paper (edge-conditioned low-rank graph convolution, rank 8), trained on
Tarbert layers of SPE10 with one-day steps and rolled out autoregressively to day 1825 (training used days up to 1000).
**Imposed**: the simulator's well volumes drive the state model. **Rate**: the rate model predicts the well volumes
from the state. The test sets are 20 cases on 32x32 Tarbert grids (two of them with a single producer and no injector,
1P0I), 15 on 60x60 grids and 15 on 120x60 grids (grids not seen in training), and 15 Upper Ness (fluvial) 32x32 cases
evaluated with the same model, without fine-tuning. Case names: `L<layer>_<grid>_<patch>_<producers>P<injectors>I_<fixed|variable>`
(BHP schedule: fixed or variable in time).

## How the results were produced

The rollouts were computed once with the models above on the test simulations (IMEX reference runs); the files they
wrote (predicted and simulated fields, well volumes) are the source of every figure and metric here. The figures are
made by `resflow.plotting` with the style of the paper figures (maps: `imshow` with the origin at the lower left, columns
are i and rows are j, cells of 50 m; wells as triangles, producer white pointing up, injector black pointing down). The
metrics are the ones of the paper tables, computed on the cell fields of each rollout. Because the rollouts ran on a
GPU with non-deterministic scatter operations, a repeated rollout differs slightly from these files; the numbers here
are the published draw. Colour scales are set per map row (shared by simulator and proxy), error scales are symmetric.

## Units and definitions

Pressure in MPa (the rollout files hold kgf/cm2, converted with 0.0980665); permeability in md; saturation
dimensionless; volumes in sm3 (figures in 10^3 sm3); time in days. P R2: coefficient of determination of the pressure
over all cells (clipped at -1 only in the medians of `summary.csv`, not in `all_rollouts.csv` or `metrics.csv`). Shifted
P R2: after removing the mean offset between the two pressure maps. MAE: mean absolute error over the cells. |MBe|:
absolute material-balance error of the phase, in % (definition as in the paper). Cumulative errors
(rate mode): absolute error of the field's cumulative oil production, water production (relative to the true cumulative
water production) and water injection, in %. Sw is not scored for 1P0I cases (maps are still drawn).

`metrics.csv` columns: `mode, day, P_r2, P_shifted_r2, Sw_r2, P_mae_MPa, Sw_mae, MBe_oil_pct, MBe_water_pct` and, for
the rate mode, `cum_oil_prod_abs_err_pct, cum_water_prod_abs_err_pct, cum_water_inj_abs_err_pct`.
